Your mind map becomes
your business plan.

With one button, send the business ideas you sorted out in a mind map to a Hangul (HWP) business plan or report, a PPT, or a Word document, and keep writing in the same window. Change one side and the other follows. For AI, Hanpan first uses the AI already installed on your PC.

Download for Windows Free · Windows 10/11 (64-bit) · v0.22.0 · All versions Mac beta: Apple silicon (M1 or later) · Intel

Think, write, and design on one board

Mind map, Hangul, Word, PPT, Excel, and PDF open as tabs in one window. Copying and pasting between programs is now one button.

Home screen

Type a topic and start with a mind map

Type the topic you want to work out on the start board at the top of Home and press Create mind map. A new mind map opens right away with that topic in the center.

  • Cards follow the order of the work: Mind map → AI HWP → AI PowerPoint → Hanpan templates
  • Word, Excel, design, and PDF tools gathered in one row below
Mind map → documents

One mind map to a business plan, report, PPT, and Word document

Press Send in the toolbar and the document opens in a new tab in the same window.

  • Korean bullet-style reports (□ ○ ―), chapter reports (Ⅰ.), and Korean government document numbering (1. 가. 1))
  • Use the words in your mind map as they are, or let AI expand them into bullet-style sentences
  • One main branch becomes one slide, and notes become speaker notes
PPT slide styles

Pick from 10 styles for your presentation

Pick a style when you send to PPT, and the cover, contents, body, and closing slides are all designed in that style.

  • Big one-line message, Conclusion-first report, Plain-language cards, Consulting framework, Story-driven talk, Color-accent info, Minimal blocks, Warm personal story, Balanced template, Investor pitch deck
  • Laid out as cards, matrices, timelines, or big numbers to fit what each branch holds
  • After it is made, you can still edit text, colors, and shapes in the PPT tab
Markdown converter

Markdown to Hangul, Word, and PDF in one go

Paste Markdown from text an AI wrote for you or from your notes and press Convert all. The formats you picked are created together in one folder.

  • Pick only what you need from HWP, HWPX, Word, ODT, PDF, and HTML
  • Report, general document, or Korean government document layout, with tables as real tables
  • Check the document layout in a preview before you create it
Two-way sync

Edit the mind map and the document follows, edit the document and the mind map follows

After you send it, the mind map and the Hangul document stay linked. Hanpan shows you what changed first, and where the two sides were edited differently, you choose. Body text you wrote in the document is kept.

Tools for Korean official documents and reports

From tidying official documents to three-color review

  • Dates as 2026. 5. 8., times as 14:30, and 끝. ("End.") at the end of the body
  • Checks for recipient and title, item numbering order, and mixed sentence endings
  • Change the tone for readers in their 60s, 40s, or the MZ generation
  • Highlighters for fact checks (red), key points (blue), and numbers (yellow)
  • 19 templates for official documents, reports, and proposals
AI Design

Notices, posters, and card news from one line to AI

Write what you want to make in one line in ✦ AI Assistant on the right. The AI writes the text, and Hanpan produces a design with the layout and colors matched. You can also fill a template you picked with just your own content.

  • A4 notices, card news, thumbnails, presentation screens, vertical stories
  • After it is made, edit text and shapes one by one, and select a text box to polish, shorten, or translate it from the ✦ bar
  • Select several and move them along guides, rotate, gradients, shadows, outlines, shapes, and QR codes
  • 20 templates, each with its own colors and artwork, 124 line icons, and Hanpan fonts that look the same on any PC
  • Change the board size and the layout stays: turn an A4 notice into card news or a thumbnail
  • Group and move together, crop photos, draw handwriting with a pen, rulers, and add your own font files
  • Save as PNG, JPG, or PDF, or copy and paste straight into Hangul, Word, or PPT

Install

No sign-up and no payment. Documents are opened and saved only on your PC.

  1. Download

    Download and run Hanpan-Setup-0.22.0.exe. We have applied to SignPath Foundation for free code signing for open source projects for the Windows installer (Free code signing provided by SignPath.io, certificate by SignPath Foundation). The signing policy is in the Code signing policy.

  2. Get past the warnings

    If Chrome or Edge stops the file with a warning such as "Unverified download" or "This file isn't commonly downloaded", open the downloads list (Ctrl+J) and press Keep for that file (in Edge, also Keep anyway). If the file is still named "Unconfirmed ….crdownload", you have not done this step yet.

    If a "Windows protected your PC" window appears, press More info and then Run anyway. This message appears because it is a free program that has not received code signing yet.

  3. Start Hanpan

    On first launch you agree to the terms of use and create a security key. We recommend creating it on a USB drive; if you do not have one, you can create it on this PC. Then the Home screen opens. Press User manual at the top of the Home screen or press F1 to see how to use Hanpan in Getting started with Hanpan.

When a new version is out, Hanpan tells you and updates itself.

Install on Mac (beta)

Download the file for your Mac: Apple silicon (M1 or later) · Intel. In the window that opens, drag Hanpan into the Applications folder.

If an "unidentified developer" message appears the first time you open it, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and press Open Anyway. This message appears because it is a free program without an Apple developer signature. On Mac, Hanpan cannot update itself, so when it tells you a new version is out, download it again and replace the app.

If you see "'Hanpan' is damaged and can't be opened", press Cancel, not Move to Trash. The file is not broken; macOS is blocking an unsigned app downloaded from the internet. After moving Hanpan to the Applications folder, paste the line below into Terminal (search "Terminal" in Spotlight), press Return, and open Hanpan again.

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Hanpan.app

AI starts with what is on your PC

On first start, Hanpan looks for AI running on this PC and uses it by default. Documents that are hard to show outside, like business plans, are not sent off your PC, so companies and public institutions can use it with peace of mind. If you want to use a Claude or ChatGPT subscription, choose it in Settings.
