# Getting started with Hanpan

Hanpan sends what you sorted out in a mind map to a Hangul (HWP) report, a slide deck or a Word report in the same window, and keeps the map and the document in step. The AI on this PC comes first.

## Send a mind map to a document

1. In the mind map toolbar, press **Send to a document** and pick a HWP report, PowerPoint or a Word report. For PowerPoint, **Slide style** offers ten designs, from Steve Jobs and McKinsey to a startup pitch deck.
2. Choose the layout (point-form report, report with chapters, official letter numbering), the date and department, and whether to send **exactly what the map says** or **let the AI write the sentences**. For PowerPoint, **Let the AI write the slides in this style** writes the titles, slide lines, supporting lines and speaker notes in the chosen style; fill in **Audience** to aim them at the people you will present to. Figures and names the map does not hold are never made up, and what needs checking goes to the speaker notes.
3. Press **Send**: the document opens in a new tab of the same window. Its first save asks where to keep it.

## Editing the mind map

- The band above the map has the **Home**, **Insert**, **Format** and **View** tabs. Ctrl+F finds topic titles on every sheet, and notes too if you tick it; Ctrl+H replaces. Going to a topic inside a folded branch unfolds the branch.
- **Insert › Picture** puts a PNG, JPG or GIF picture (10 MB at most) on the selected topic, or takes off the one it has. **Format › Sort** puts the branches of the selected topic in alphabetical order; press it again for the reverse order.
- **View › Levels** unfolds the map down to level 1, 2 or 3, and **View › Show** hides notes, labels, links, icons or pictures on screen only. The file keeps them all.
- **Insert › Icons** opens the icon panel: priority, progress, flags, stars, smileys and arrows. A topic takes one icon of each kind; press one it has to take it off. Click an icon on the map to swap it for another of its kind or remove it.
- The **Style** tab changes how the selected topics look: shape, fill, outline, font, bold, italic, underline, text colour and branch line colour. **Style › Format painter** copies the look of one topic to the next topic you click; double-click it to keep it on until Esc. **Home › Boundary** draws a box round the selected sibling topics; double-click the box to name it, or click it and press Delete to remove it.

## Sync the map and the document

- Press **Send to a document › Sync with linked documents** to see what changed in the map and in the HWP document.
- Changes made in the map go to the document, and changes made in the document come to the map. Where both sides changed a topic differently, choose the map's, the document's, or later.
- Sentences written in the document are kept. Word and PowerPoint documents can be sent but not synced back yet.

## Hanpan templates

- The **Hanpan templates** card on Home opens 22 templates (official letters, reports, proposals, minutes, plans) as HWP or Word.
- In the **Markdown converter** card on Home, paste Markdown or open a .md file and press **Convert all**: every format you ticked (HWP, HWPX, Word, ODT, PDF, HTML) is made side by side in one folder.
- The **교육** (education) group has a lesson plan for looking at old Korean paintings, an exhibition notice and a reading lesson on 「운수 좋은 날」. The pictures come from Gongu (gongu.copyright.or.kr) and are out of copyright or under CC BY, so anyone may adapt them; each one goes into the document with its credit line under it. Leave that line in. **Open as mind map** on a template card opens its headings and lists as branches of a mind map. In the slide editor, **Insert › Sound Effects** puts one of six Korean traditional-music sound effects (주식회사 아이티앤, CC BY) on the slide, with the credit in the sound’s name.
- The **서식** (official forms) group has forms attached to Korean statutes (a freedom-of-information request, an administrative appeal, a power of attorney for civil petitions and an objection to a refused petition), exactly as the National Law Information Center (law.go.kr) publishes them. **Open as HWP** opens a copy in a HWP tab; fill in the boxes, then save or print it. Statutory forms are not protected by copyright, so anyone may use them. Forms change when the law does, so check on law.go.kr that yours is the current version before you hand it in.

## Hanpan tools

- **Hanpan tools** in the strip above a HWP tab tidy and check an official letter, change its tone for a generation, mark a three-colour review and build a structured prompt.
- Tools that change the document show the result in the tab right away. Closing without saving leaves the file as it was.

## Design

- The **Design** card on Home makes posters, card news, notices and thumbnails.
- Press **Copy to paste in a document**, then Ctrl+V in a HWP, Word or PowerPoint tab to put the page in as a picture. Pages also save as PNG and PDF.
- **Send to a document › Send to card news** in a mind map makes one card per main branch.
- Press **✦ AI helper** at the top right of the design window to open the helper on the right. Under **Make new**, describe what you want and send it: the AI you chose in Settings writes the words and Hanpan lays them out as an editable design. **Fill this design** keeps the current layout and colours and rewrites only the text boxes with your content; after picking a template, **✦ Fill this template with my content** does the same. With a text box selected, a ✦ bar above it polishes, shortens, makes it catchier or translates between English and Korean in one click. Every result has **Undo**, and if no AI is connected yet you can connect one right in the helper.
- In the design window, the **Templates**, **Elements** and **Text** tabs add templates, shapes, QR codes and text styles. Shift-click or drag across empty space to pick several, move them along the guides, resize and turn them by their handles, and double-click a text box to type in place. The right side sets gradients, shadows, outlines and photo filters, the **Layers** tab sets order and locks, and **Resize** changes the page size keeping the layout. Pages also save as JPG.
- Pick several and press **Ctrl+G** to group them so they pick and move together; **Ctrl+Shift+G** ungroups. Right-click an element or empty space for copy, paste, order and lock. Double-click a photo and drag to choose what shows, and zoom it under **Crop** on the right. **Draw** on the **Elements** tab draws handwriting or highlighter strokes, the **Rulers** button at the top measures positions, and dragging with Space held moves the view. Fonts added with **Add a font file…** in the font list are saved inside the design file.
- The **Templates** tab has 20 samples, among them a notice card, a school letter, an event poster, a recruiting notice, a menu board, a certificate and a slide cover. They use the fonts that come with Hanpan (Pretendard, Noto Serif KR, Gowun Batang, Black Han Sans, Do Hyeon and more), so they look the same on any PC. Under **Icons** on the **Elements** tab, search for a word such as "calendar" or "place" to add a line icon, then change its colour and line width on the right.
- The **Free fonts from Gongu** group in the **Font** list has Noto Sans KR, Seoul Namsan, Munhwajae Dolbom, Gyeonggi Title and the Hakgyoansim Poster, Chilpanjiugae and Siganpyo fonts. They come with Hanpan, so exported PNG and PDF files keep them, and they also appear in the Word, Excel and PowerPoint font lists. They were released by the Seoul Metropolitan Government, Gyeonggi Province and the Korea Heritage Service under the Korea Open Government License type 1, and by KERIS and Google with Adobe under the SIL OFL, and can be downloaded free of charge from Gongu (gongu.copyright.or.kr).

## Pictures from Gongu

- Find out-of-copyright old paintings and photos in Gongu, the Korea Copyright Commission's library, and insert them. Get a free key from the Public Data Portal (data.go.kr) by applying for the Commission's expired-works service, enter it on the **Gongu pictures** card in **Settings › Integrations** and press **Check**. You can search up to 1,000 times a day.
- In the design window, type a title or an artist's name in **Find in Gongu** on the **Elements** tab, pick a thumbnail and press **Insert**: a credit line goes in under the picture. Keep **Add a picture credit list** on when you export to list the credits at the end, and use **Check credits** to find pictures that have none.
- In the HWP and mind map editors, ask the AI something like "find a tiger painting in Gongu and insert it". Hanpan, not the AI, writes the credit: under the picture and in a "picture credits" list at the end of an HWP document, and in the topic's note in a mind map.
- **Hanpan tools › Check picture credits** in the HWP tab and **Send to a document (HWP, PPT, Word) › Check picture credits** in a mind map list the pictures with no credit or with conditions of use.
- A search sends only the search words and your key to the Public Data Portal, never your document. Turning off **Let AI use internet tools** in Settings turns off the AI's Gongu search too. Leave the credit lines in place.

## The AI on this PC

- At first start Hanpan looks for an AI running on this PC (Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM) and, if you have not chosen an AI, uses it.
- To use another AI, choose it in **Settings › AI**.
